THE STUDIO

Made for finding what
the game actually contains.

01

Asset browser

Search the shipped packages, inspect dependencies, and preview meshes, materials and textures without losing context.

02

Animation & FBX

Decode Havok animation, preserve skeleton bindings and sockets, then round-trip validated FBX into Blender or UE5.

03

Walkable levels

Render the compiled BSP world with placed objects, real material UVs, lights, camera controls and verified baked-light atlas data.

04

Audio research

Browse every discovered WAV, listen in-app, and keep English assets separate from language-specific tracks.

NO BLACK BOXES

Evidence before guesswork.

Every reader is checked against original BioShock Remastered bytes. The project calls out uncertainty openly, validates parsers against the whole installed game, and uses rendered output to catch the mistakes that counts alone cannot see.

FROM PACKAGE TO DCC

  1. 01Read the original .bsm package
  2. 02Resolve geometry, materials, audio and dependencies
  3. 03Inspect or preview in the desktop studio
  4. 04Export to Blender, FBX, PNG, DDS, WAV and scene data
